## Featured research

### [Bearing fault diagnosis and generalization](https://github.com/alm-82/cwru-bearing-generalization)

My undergraduate research examines bearing-fault classification when operating load or fault size changes. The workflow progresses from vibration waveforms, FFT and Hilbert envelope analysis through classical features and SVM baselines to STFT spectrograms and EfficientNet-B0.

Saved historical evaluations report **98.97% cross-load accuracy** and **70.00% cross-fault-size accuracy** under different experimental protocols; these are not a controlled comparison. Historical model weights are unavailable, and full training has not been independently reproduced. The repository documents the acquisition-rate mismatch, healthy-record reuse, and other limitations.
